About

I got into software the way most engineers do by trying to fix something, then falling for the craft of building. What started with tinkering became a full practice across web development, AI systems, and automation, and eventually a studio of my own.

Today I split my time between two things: as Founder of 45Creatives, I design and build websites, dashboards, and brand systems for clients who want their business to look and perform like a category leader, not a template. And independently, I build AI-driven trading systems, backend platforms, and automation tools that solve real operational problems, from billing systems for internet providers to algorithmic strategies for financial markets.

What ties it together is the same instinct: understand the problem deeply, choose the simplest architecture that solves it well, and sweat the details most people skip, including accessibility, performance, clean code, and a UI that feels considered rather than assembled.
